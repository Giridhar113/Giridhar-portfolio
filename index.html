Professional Summary

I'm Giridhar Reddy Parlapalli, an Associate Software Engineer focused on building clean, responsive, and user-friendly web interfaces. I work with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript to turn structured content into polished digital experiences.

My Electronics and Communication Engineering background gives me strong fundamentals in programming, debugging, IoT, and logical problem solving. I bring that discipline into frontend work through careful layout, clear visual hierarchy, and reliable implementation.
